01 / About

A decade of native iOS — from ATM-grade banking to indie products.

I'm a senior iOS engineer who's spent the last nine years turning ambiguous briefs into beautiful, performant native apps. I've shipped products for digital banks, audio hardware companies, telcos, and consumer brands — and I now build my own under the As Apps studio.

I lead with curiosity, ship with discipline, and care deeply about the small details users feel but rarely articulate — haptics that land, animations that breathe, code that holds up under pressure.

  • Native by default

    Swift, SwiftUI, UIKit. I pick the boring tool when it ships better, the new tool when it earns its place.

  • Architecture that scales

    MVVM, VIPER, Clean Swift — chosen for the team and the lifetime of the product, not the slide deck.

  • Ships, not promises

    CI/CD on Bitrise, Azure, Fastlane, GitHub Actions. From first commit to App Store, with confidence.

  • Cross-functional by habit

    Trilingual (EN / TR / FA). I work directly with designers, product, hardware, and stakeholders.
